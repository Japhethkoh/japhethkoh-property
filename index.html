About Japheth

Steady progress.
Better decisions.

A property journey is personal. My role is to understand what matters to you and help you take the next step with confidence.

I’m Japheth Koh, a passionate property negotiator based in Johor Bahru, representing The Roof Realty. My journey in the property industry has been one of continuous learning, challenges, growth and breakthroughs.

Coming from humble beginnings, I’ve learned the value of hard work, perseverance and never giving up when things get tough. I believe success takes discipline, consistency, resilience and the courage to keep moving forward.

My mission is more than selling properties. It’s about understanding people’s needs, building trust, creating opportunities and helping clients make the right property decisions. Whether you’re buying, selling, renting or investing, every journey deserves commitment, honesty and professional service.

“I want to become better every day, create greater value for my clients and make a positive difference in the property industry.”
